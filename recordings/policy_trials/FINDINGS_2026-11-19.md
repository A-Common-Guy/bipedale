## policies_20261119-073801 (95 s of `rl_agile_step`, camera on)

The first trial with the camera running (`camera:=true`, `/terrain_profile`
at 22 Hz in the bag) and with `safety.oscillation_hold_s` 1.2. No wheel
filter. Driven to 1 m/s; 68 s of the 95 driving.

**Hops.** Eight requested (`/cmd_vel` `linear.y` 0.05) at 51.0, 54.5, 66.9,
68.8, 77.3, 92.1, 93.0 and 94.1 s. None tripped the oscillation watchdog,
which cut every hop the evening before. The controller's own reading
(`oscillation_leg_rad_s`, 50 Hz diagnostics) peaks at 1.95-2.33 rad/s per hop
and is beyond the 1.0 limit for 0.70-0.80 s after a single hop, as in
simulation (2.2-2.4 rad/s, 0.7-0.8 s). Hops less than 2 s apart add up: 1.1
to 1.7 s beyond the limit within 3 s (66.9 + 68.8, 92.1 + 93.0). That did not
trip the 1.2 s hold here, so the stretches were not continuous, but the
margin for hops in quick succession is thin. Pitch stays within -16 to +10
degrees through a hop.

**The end: a drive fault, not a fall.** At 94.6 s, half a second after the
third hop in 2 s at 1 m/s (pitch at +21 degrees), both wheel drives report
`axis error 0x40 flags=9` and the hardware goes down. The same code stopped
`left_wheel` 46 s into the previous run of the same session (before this
recording) and `left_wheel` and `right_motor_inner` on 2026-11-18, each time
with `rl_agile_step` hopping. The drives are at 31-36 C and report no error
until that moment; the largest measured wheel torque in the recording is
7.9 Nm, leg torque 13.7 Nm. What 0x40 with flags 9 means in the drive's
firmware is not decoded in this repository. Two drives failing in the same
cycle points at something they share, the supply during a landing, rather
than at one motor; that is a guess, not a measurement (the bag has no bus
voltage).

**Standing.** 0.07 rad/s of pitch at 12 Hz (typical 2 s window; quiet is
below 0.06, the evening before 0.12 without the camera).

**The profile looks too high.** Where known, 47 % of the samples are more
than 2 cm off the floor, the far half (0.8-1.35 m) reads 4 to 12 cm on
average over every 10 s of the run, and the clip (+25 cm) is reached
throughout. The node's floor fit reads pitch +1.5 degrees, roll +0.3, offset
-4 mm mid-run. The recording cannot tell walls and furniture ahead (which
read as their height from their face on) from a profile that rises with
distance because of the mount; 1.5 degrees is 3.5 cm at 1.35 m. Known points:
62-99 % per sample, lowest at 0.1-0.2 m ahead of the left wheel. A policy
that stops before anything taller than 6 cm will hesitate on such a profile:
check it standing on a flat, empty floor before judging the policy.
